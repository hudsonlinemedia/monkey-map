👆Pick a country on the map and a local monkey will pop up with its primate stats.
Music is synthesized live in your browser: short original loops built around each country's traditional and Indigenous instruments. They aren't recordings of real performances.
Species counts are approximate and shift with taxonomy (they count species; Brazil's figure includes subspecies). Sources: IUCN Primate fact sheet; IUCN/SSC Primate Specialist Group via Primate Conservation (Peru); ((o))eco 2026 (Brazil); lists of primates by country. Map borders are simplified for play.
